My Vocabulary - Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 2026

1. Overview

My Vocabulary ("we", "our", or "app") provides a vocabulary learning application. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data when you use the app, including Google Sign-In and Cloud Synchronization features powered by Google Firebase.

2. Information We Collect

When you sign in using your Google Account, we collect the following information:

  • Email Address & Display Name: Obtained via Google Sign-In to manage your account.
  • Firebase User ID (UID): A unique identifier generated by Google Firebase Authentication.
  • App Data: Your vocabulary dictionaries and word lists are uploaded and synced to Google Cloud Firestore so you can access them across your devices.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information strictly to:

  • Authenticate your account and keep you signed in securely.
  • Sync your vocabulary dictionaries and practice progress across your devices.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties or advertisers.

4. Third-Party Services

We use trusted Google services to power app features:

  • Google Sign-In / Credential Manager: For secure authentication.
  • Firebase Authentication & Firestore: For user account management and encrypted cloud data storage.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: For collecting anonymous crash reports, stack traces, and diagnostic data to improve app stability and fix bugs.

All data transmitted between your device and Google servers is encrypted in transit using industry-standard HTTPS/TLS encryption.

5. Account & Data Deletion Request

You have the right to request the deletion of your account and all associated cloud dictionaries at any time.

How to request deletion:

  • To permanently delete your account and all synced vocabulary data from our cloud servers, please send an email request to our support team with the subject line "Account Deletion Request".
  • Upon receiving your request, all your stored dictionaries and user profile records will be permanently removed from Google Firebase within 30 days.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact the developer.
